Entry for the [make.fun bounty "The Funniest Honest Appliance Error"](https://make.fun/c/u-xvader-the-funniest-honest-appliance-error-adec4450): one line of at most 200 characters, the funniest honest error message an appliance could show.

## The entry

> ERROR: The plate is hot. The food is cold. I heated what I could reach.

A microwave, reporting the most universal microwave failure there is: it heats unevenly, the plate soaks up the energy, and the food stays cold. The laugh is the machine's deadpan excuse — it did heat *something*, just what it could reach.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) validates the line — one line, 1–200 characters, no control characters — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
